What's Inside CashCraft

Smart POS Billing

Lightning-fast billing at the counter. Add items, apply discounts, accept multiple payment modes, and print receipts instantly.

Inventory Management

Track every product in your store — stock levels, purchase prices, selling prices, low stock alerts, and category management.

Sales Analytics

Detailed dashboards showing daily, weekly and monthly sales, top-selling products, profit margins, and business trends.

Customer Management

Maintain customer profiles, track purchase history, manage loyalty points, and record outstanding dues with ease.

Invoice & Receipts

Generate professional, print-ready invoices and thermal receipts with your business branding and all transaction details.

100% Offline First

Works completely without internet. All your data stays local and secure on your Windows machine — no subscription needed.

Product Categories

Organize products into custom categories and subcategories for fast search and a clean, structured inventory workflow.

Secure Access Control

Role-based login ensures only authorized staff can access sensitive reports, refund features, and admin settings.

Easy Setup

Simple installer, zero configuration complexity. Get up and running in minutes — even for non-technical shop owners.
