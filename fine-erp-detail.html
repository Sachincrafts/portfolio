What's Inside

Jewellery Inventory

Track gold, silver, diamond, and other jewellery with weight, purity, making charges, and real-time stock levels.

GST-Ready Billing

Generate professional invoices with automatic GST calculation, HSMC codes, and print-ready PDF output.

Barter & Pawn

Manage gold barter and pawn transactions with full ledger tracking, interest calculations and due alerts.

Customer Management

Complete customer profiles with purchase history, outstanding balances, and personalized communication.

Sales Analytics

Powerful dashboards with daily, weekly, monthly sales reports and profit analysis at a glance.

Cloud Backup

Automatic cloud sync keeps your data safe and accessible across multiple devices and locations.
